Atelier Disclaimer

This Atelier Disclaimer provides essential guidance regarding textile characteristics, bespoke fitting timelines, and educational fashion treatises published by Moda Frock Cove.

1. Purpose and Scope of Governance

The fashion treatises, historical textile commentaries, pattern analyses, and garment care recommendations published on this website are provided solely for general educational, historical, and aesthetic appreciation. While Moda Frock Cove endeavors to ensure the factual precision of all historical references and textile specifications, we make no express or implied warranties regarding the completeness or absolute universal applicability of our published methods. Reliance on any textile guidance or historical fashion analysis presented on this platform is undertaken strictly at the reader's discretion.

2. Atelier Obligations and Client Rights

Bespoke dressmaking is an inherently individualized artistic process wherein garment drape, balance, and fit are tailored specifically to unique personal posture and body geometry. Garments depicted in our online lookbooks and journal articles reflect specific client commissions and archival museum pieces; actual custom results will vary based on individual measurements, chosen silk weights, and structural boning selections. Nothing on this website constitutes a binding guarantee of uniform visual outcome for differing body proportions without personal muslin fitting sessions.

3. Intellectual Assets and Permitted Use

Natural fibers such as mulberry silk, Belgian flax linen, and Scottish wool exhibit natural organic irregularities, slubs, and delicate dye variations that constitute the authentic hallmarks of fine weaving rather than manufacturing defects. Moda Frock Cove is not liable for natural variations in fabric hand, weave texture, or subtle dye lot differences inherent to small-batch European textile production. Clients are encouraged to inspect physical fabric swatches during their private consultation before finalizing their bespoke material orders.

4. Operational Protocols and Data Management

Garment care and preservation guidelines published in our journal are based on professional museum conservation practices and are intended for informational reference. Because environmental storage conditions, humidity levels, atmospheric pollutants, and garment wear histories vary widely, Moda Frock Cove accepts no liability for textile degradation resulting from improper self-cleaning, consumer laundering mistakes, or unauthorized alterations performed outside our Manhattan atelier.

5. Revisions and Statutory Compliance

Moda Frock Cove reserves the absolute right to revise, update, or remove any published fashion article, portfolio presentation, or atelier policy without prior public notice. We are not responsible for temporary website interruptions or typographical errors that may occur within our digital archives. All client relationships and custom dressmaking agreements are governed exclusively by formal bespoke commission contracts executed directly between the client and our Manhattan atelier.
