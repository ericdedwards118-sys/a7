Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Moda Frock Cove utilizes functional, analytical, and preference cookies to deliver an elegant and secure digital browsing experience.

1. Purpose and Scope of Governance

This Cookie Policy outlines the transparent practices of Moda Frock Cove regarding the use of cookies and related web technologies on our official website. When you access our digital collections and couture treatises, small alphanumeric data files known as cookies may be saved to your device to enhance navigation responsiveness and remember your display preferences. We are deeply committed to maintaining transparent digital operations, utilizing cookies only to support essential site functionality, enhance user navigation flow, and analyze aggregate traffic without compromising client anonymity.

2. Atelier Obligations and Client Rights

Essential cookies are strictly required to enable core digital functions on our platform, such as securing client appointment forms, maintaining active session tokens, and routing web requests efficiently. Without these indispensable files, our appointment submission workflows and interactive atelier configurators cannot operate reliably. Because essential cookies are necessary for fundamental platform operation and security, they are deployed automatically upon visiting our website and do not collect behavioral tracking data for third-party commercial exploitation.

3. Intellectual Assets and Permitted Use

Analytical and performance cookies help our digital team evaluate how visitors interact with our published couture treatises, lookbooks, and navigational menus. By collecting aggregated and anonymized telemetry on page loading speeds, bounce rates, and popular fashion topics, these cookies allow us to refine our educational resources and improve user accessibility across desktop and mobile devices. None of the statistical performance data collected through these analytical tools is linked to your confidential personal client profile.

4. Operational Protocols and Data Management

Preference cookies allow our digital platform to remember your individualized browsing settings, such as your preferred typography scaling, interactive configurator states, and past journal selections. These cookies enhance your browsing ease by eliminating the need to re-enter preferences upon each visit, creating a tailored and seamless digital experience. You maintain complete control over preference cookies and may choose to disable them at any time through your individual web browser settings.

5. Revisions and Statutory Compliance

You have the absolute right to accept, configure, or block cookies through your individual browser controls at any time. Most modern browsers allow you to inspect stored cookies, delete existing records, or configure automatic blocking for third-party domains. Please note that restricting certain functional cookies may impact the dynamic responsiveness of our interactive atelier features. For further inquiries regarding our cookie management protocols, please contact our technical team at our Manhattan atelier address.
