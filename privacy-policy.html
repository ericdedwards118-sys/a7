Atelier Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Moda Frock Cove collects, maintains, and protects confidential client information gathered during bespoke consultations and digital inquiries.

1. Purpose and Scope of Governance

At Moda Frock Cove, protecting the confidentiality of our bespoke clientele is a cornerstone of our service philosophy. When you schedule a private fitting at our Mercer Street salon or submit bespoke measurements through our secure digital portals, we collect only the essential personal details required to deliver customized dressmaking services. This information includes your full legal name, private contact details, bespoke anatomical measurements, garment fitting histories, and specific textile preferences. We treat every client record with the highest degree of discretion, applying rigorous technical safeguards and encryption protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

2. Atelier Obligations and Client Rights

The personal data you share with Moda Frock Cove is used exclusively to facilitate private salon fittings, maintain custom dress patterns, process bespoke commissions, and provide individualized couture updates. We never monetize, rent, disclose, or trade client identities or personal measurements with third-party advertising brokers or commercial marketing networks under any circumstances. Trusted administrative partners who assist in operating our secure customer portals are bound by strict non-disclosure obligations, ensuring your data remains protected throughout your engagement with our fashion atelier.

3. Intellectual Assets and Permitted Use

Our online platform uses minimal technical cookies and analytics tools solely to optimize browsing speeds, maintain navigation preferences, and evaluate website performance metrics. These non-intrusive technologies do not track your activity across unrelated third-party websites and do not store sensitive personal information without your explicit permission. You retain full autonomy to modify your browser preferences to restrict cookie storage at any time, although disabling certain functional session cookies may alter the interactive presentation of our bespoke dressmaking catalog.

4. Operational Protocols and Data Management

Moda Frock Cove respects your sovereign legal rights regarding your personal records in full accordance with international data protection frameworks. You may at any time request a comprehensive copy of the personal measurements and contact records archived within our atelier registry, request factual corrections to your client profile, or request permanent deletion of your historical data. To exercise any of these privacy rights, please direct your formal request to our dedicated privacy liaison at our official Manhattan salon address or via our verified concierge desk.

5. Revisions and Statutory Compliance

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect evolving legal mandates, enhanced data protection technologies, and refined salon operating standards. Any formal modifications to our privacy practices will be published directly on this official page with an updated regulatory revision timestamp. Continued engagement with our bespoke salon services and online digital platforms following published changes signifies your informed acknowledgment of our updated confidentiality provisions and ongoing privacy standards.
